## Purpose

Because this project was completed in a simulated lab environment without a production file server, local folders and fictional documents were used to demonstrate the data-preservation process.

## Preservation Actions

1. Identified the simulated employee data location.
2. Reviewed the files associated with the employee.
3. Created a simulated HR records archive location.
4. Copied the three fictional business documents to the archive.
5. Confirmed that the archived files were present.
6. Confirmed that the original files remained intact.
7. No original business data was deleted.

The simulated business data was successfully preserved.

This demonstrates the distinction between **removing an employee's access** and **deleting the employee's business data**.
